Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Introduction and Scope

MaraLyrics ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of the people who use our services. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and share it, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have.

It applies to our website at maralyrics.com and its associated subdomains (the "Site"), to the accounts you can create on the Site, and to the MaraLyrics Android app available on Google Play (the "App"), together the "Services". Where the App collects information, this policy applies to it as well; the App's Google Play listing contains a "Data safety" summary.

For the personal information described here, MaraLyrics is the "controller" (the party that decides how and why it is used). You can reach us at info@maralyrics.com. Where the law requires your consent (for example for non-essential cookies), we ask for it separately; using the Services does not by itself count as consent to anything that requires an opt-in.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

We collect personal information that you give us when you:

  • Use a form: contact and feedback or error-report forms (name, email address, message and the song concerned)
  • Create an account: email address and password (we store only a salted, one-way hash of the password, never the password itself); optionally a username, display name and profile photo; a phone number, which is optional for readers and required if you want to claim an artist or composer profile
  • Sign in with Google: Google sends us your Google account identifier, email address and name. We never see your Google password
  • Claim an artist or composer profile: the explanation you write of how we can verify you, and later the profile content you edit (biography, photo, social links). Once a claim is approved, that profile content is shown publicly on the profile page
  • Order a "Green mark": the plan you choose, the payment reference you enter, an optional note and an optional image of your receipt. Payment itself is made outside the Site using the instructions shown when you order; we do not receive or store your card number or bank login details
  • Contribute content: lyrics, chords, corrections or other material you send us

2.2 Information Created When You Use an Account

If you are signed in, we store the songs you mark as favorites or pin, the list of songs you have downloaded for offline use, and your preferences (theme, language, list view and default calendar), so that they stay the same on every device where you sign in. We also keep a record that a confirmation or password-reset email was sent to you (the link itself is stored only as a one-way hash).

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

When you access the Services, we and our service providers may automatically collect:

  • Browser type and version, operating system, device type and screen resolution
  • Pages visited, time spent on pages and the referring website address
  • Your IP address. We use it for security (for example to limit repeated failed sign-in attempts) and our hosting provider processes it to deliver and protect the Services

2.4 Information Stored on Your Device

The Services store small amounts of data in your browser or device: your language and theme, your favorites, the sign-in session when you are signed in, your cookie choice, the key you picked for a song's guitar chords, and, if you download songs, the songs themselves and the site's files so they work offline. See section 3.

3. Cookies, Local Storage & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies (such as your browser's local storage and offline cache) in these groups:

  • Strictly necessary: keep you signed in, remember your cookie choice and protect forms and sign-in with Cloudflare Turnstile (a bot check). These do not need your consent.
  • Preferences: your language, theme, favorites, sort order and similar settings. If you choose "Essential only" on our cookie banner, we clear the optional preference items we stored.
  • Analytics: we may use privacy-focused analytics provided by our infrastructure provider (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics) to understand how the Services are used. These tools collect aggregated usage data.
  • Advertising: we display advertisements through third-party vendors, including Google. These vendors use cookies (such as the Google Ads cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this Site and other websites, so that the ads shown to you are more relevant. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices. Advertising cookies are set by those vendors, so they are controlled through those settings and through your browser, not through our cookie banner.

You can control cookies and stored data through your browser settings and by clearing site data. Disabling them may affect features such as staying signed in, favorites and offline downloads.

4. Why We Use Your Information (Legal Bases)

Where data-protection law such as the GDPR applies, we rely on these legal bases:

  • Contract: to create and run your account, sync your data across devices, review profile claims and process Green mark orders you ask for
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the Services secure, prevent spam, abuse and fraud, understand and improve how the Services are used, and answer your messages
  • Consent: for non-essential cookies and personalized advertising, and when you choose to sign in with Google. You can withdraw consent at any time
  • Legal obligation: where we must keep or disclose information to comply with the law

5. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate, maintain, secure and improve the Services
  • Create and manage your account, confirm your email address, reset your password and keep your favorites, pinned songs, downloads and preferences in step on your devices
  • Review artist and composer profile claims and process Green mark orders
  • Respond to your inquiries and process feedback and error reports
  • Protect the Services, including limiting repeated failed sign-ins and blocking bots and spam
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve the experience, and display advertisements
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms & Conditions

We send only service emails, such as an address confirmation or a password-reset link. We do not send marketing emails.

6. Who Can See Your Information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share it only as follows:

  • Publicly: song lyrics and credits; the content of an artist or composer profile once a claim is approved (biography, photo, links); and, on the Project page, the usernames, roles and profile pictures of the site team members who edit content. Your account's email address, phone number, favorites and downloads are never shown publicly.
  • Our team: a small number of authorized administrators can see messages sent through the forms, profile-claim details (including your contact details and verification explanation) and Green mark orders (including receipts), to deal with them. Administrator access is limited by role and administrator actions are logged.
  • Service providers that process data on our behalf, listed in section 7.
  • Authorities and others where the law requires it, to protect rights, safety and security, or in connection with a reorganization of the project; we would tell you if your information were then to be governed by a different policy.

7. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services that process data when you use the Services. They have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

  • Cloudflare: hosting, database, DNS, CDN, security and, where enabled, analytics. Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a visitor is not a bot when you create an account, send a form, claim a profile, or sign in after several failed attempts; it may process your IP address and browser signals
  • Google AdSense: advertisement display and management. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this Site and/or other sites. You can learn more about how Google collects and uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Sign-In: if you choose "Continue with Google", Google handles your sign-in and tells us your account identifier, email address and name
  • Google Fonts: the Site loads its typeface from Google's servers, which receive your IP address and browser details when the page loads
  • YouTube: some pages (for example Mara Idol) show video previews. The preview image is loaded from YouTube's servers, and the video player (youtube-nocookie.com) loads only after you click play
  • Resend: sends our service emails (address confirmation and password reset), so it receives your email address and the message
  • Community calendar: events are loaded directly from the community calendar service at calendar-api.marareih.org, which receives your IP address and browser details
  • Google Play: distributes the App; Google's own terms and privacy policy apply to your use of Google Play

8. International Transfers

Our service providers operate in several countries, including the United States, so your information may be processed outside the country where you live. Where the law requires it, we rely on safeguards such as the providers' data-processing terms and standard contractual clauses or other recognized transfer mechanisms.

9. How Long We Keep Your Information

  • Account information (including synced favorites, pinned songs, downloads list and preferences): until you delete your account. Deleting it also deletes your pending claims and your Green mark orders, including any receipt you uploaded.
  • Profile content that you published on an artist or composer page remains part of that public page unless you ask us to change or remove it. An account that manages a profile cannot be deleted until the profile has been released; contact us to do that.
  • Sign-in attempt records (the name or email address and the IP address used): deleted automatically within 24 hours, and the failed-attempt limits themselves work over a 15-minute window.
  • Confirmation and reset links expire after 24 hours and 1 hour respectively and are stored only as one-way hashes.
  • Messages and reports sent through the forms: for as long as needed to handle them and for a reasonable period afterwards; you can ask us to delete them.
  • Backups: our database provider keeps short-lived backups, after which deleted data is overwritten.

10. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure servers operated by Cloudflare, Inc. We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect it: connections are encrypted (HTTPS), passwords are stored only as salted one-way hashes, repeated failed sign-ins are limited and challenged, changing your password signs your other devices out, access to personal data is restricted by role, and administrator actions are logged. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the authorities where the law requires.

11. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR/UK GDPR or in California), you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal data and receive a copy in a portable format
  • Correct inaccurate data and delete your data
  • Object to or restrict the processing of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the processing done before
  • Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information for money; to opt out of personalized advertising use the links in section 3
  • Not be treated unfairly for using these rights

If you have an account, you can do much of this yourself on the Account page: edit your profile, download everything we hold about your account as a file, and delete your account. For anything else, or if you do not have an account, contact us at info@maralyrics.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity, and we aim to reply within 30 days. If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to your local data-protection authority.

12. Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and accounts are for people aged 13 or older (or the higher minimum age in your country, up to 16, where the law sets one). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below that age. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page with an updated "Last updated" date and, for significant changes, give a more prominent notice on the Site or by email to account holders. Where the law requires your consent to a change, we will ask for it.

14. Language

This policy may be available in other languages for convenience. If a translation differs from the English version, the English version prevails.

15. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or want to exercise your rights, please contact us at:

info@maralyrics.com
