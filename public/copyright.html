Copyright & Removal Request

Our Commitment

MaraLyrics respects the intellectual property rights of songwriters, composers, and artists. Our platform exists to preserve and celebrate Mara musical heritage, and we are committed to handling all copyright matters responsibly and promptly.

Removal Request Process

If you are a copyright holder (or authorized to act on behalf of one) and believe that your copyrighted work has been posted on MaraLyrics without authorization, you may submit a removal request. Please follow the steps below:

1

Identify the Content

Provide the specific URL(s) of the page(s) containing the copyrighted material you wish to have removed.

2

Provide Your Identity

Include your full legal name, relationship to the copyrighted work (songwriter, composer, publisher, authorized agent), and contact information.

3

Include a Statement

Provide a statement that you have a good faith belief that the use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.

4

Send Your Request

Email your complete removal request to:

info@maralyrics.com

What Happens Next

  • We will acknowledge receipt of your request within 48 hours.
  • We will review and verify the claim promptly.
  • If the claim is valid, the identified content will be removed within 7 business days.
  • You will be notified once the content has been removed.

Important Notes

  • Fraudulent or bad-faith removal requests may be subject to legal consequences.
  • We may reach out for additional information or clarification before processing a request.
  • If you believe content was removed in error, you may contact us to file a counter-notification.

Contact
